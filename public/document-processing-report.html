Abstract

This Traffic Management Plan provides pre-approved Traffic Guidance Scheme (TGS) diagrams for non-complex traffic management arrangements on state roads in the Wheatbelt region of Western Australia. The document is designed for use by accredited Traffic Management Supervisors and Traffic Controllers working on MRWA-maintained roads within the Wheatbelt region. The collection covers various work scenarios including lane closures, shoulder closures, mobile works, and short-term low-impact works specific to rural road environments. The diagrams are categorized by type: Setup and Guidance (IW), Lane Closures (LC), Lateral Shifts (LS), Reversible Flow (RF), Emergency Setups (EW), and Aftercare (AC). Each diagram includes speed zone requirements ranging from 40 km/h to 110 km/h, signage schedules with appropriate T-series signs, and implementation notes for site-specific adaptation. This TMP should be used in conjunction with the MRWA Code of Practice and requires site-specific assessment by a qualified Traffic Management Supervisor.
